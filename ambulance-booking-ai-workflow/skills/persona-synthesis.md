# Persona Synthesis

## Input
input/brd.md + input/research-notes.md

## Output
output/phase-01/persona.md

## Procedure and rules
Extract role, context, goals, behaviors and constraints with sample IDs. Separate observed sample content from assumptions. Do not fabricate demographics. Include gaps and contradiction notes.

## Validation
Nonempty artifact; owner/version/input revision; evidence references resolvable; assumptions and gaps visible; consistent with BRD. Stop on missing required inputs or approval. Return failures to phase owner rather than inventing missing facts.

## Handoff
Return artifact path, validation result, sources, unresolved questions and next dependency to the owning agent. This task contract is reusable workflow documentation.
