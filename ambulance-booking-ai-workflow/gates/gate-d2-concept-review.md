# Gate D2 — Concept review

Required artifacts: hmw.md, feature-opportunities.md, concept-direction.md. Require current revisions and prior gate approval where applicable.

## Human checks
- HMWs derive from reviewed pain points
- Features trace to PP/HMW IDs
- Assumptions visible
- Concept focused
- No hypothesis presented as proven
- Scope matches BRD

## Decision and routing
Human chooses APPROVE / REVISE / REJECT in output/reviews/d2-review.md, with reviewer, date, revisions and feedback. Store APPROVE as APPROVED. Never infer approval from silence or automated validation. REVISE → Product Ideation Agent; REJECT → stop and request better input; APPROVED → Phase 3. Upstream edits invalidate approval. A PENDING or NOT_READY gate stops routing.
