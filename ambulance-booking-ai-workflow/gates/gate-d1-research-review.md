# Gate D1 — Research review

Required artifacts: persona.md, pain-points.md, competitor-notes.md. Require current revisions and prior gate approval where applicable.

## Human checks
- Persona evidence-backed within labeled samples
- Assumptions labeled
- Pain points trace to research IDs
- No unsupported facts
- Competitive observations sourced or illustrative
- Research gaps visible

## Decision and routing
Human chooses APPROVE / REVISE / REJECT in output/reviews/d1-review.md, with reviewer, date, revisions and feedback. Store APPROVE as APPROVED. Never infer approval from silence or automated validation. REVISE → Research Agent; REJECT → stop and request better input; APPROVED → Phase 2. Upstream edits invalidate approval. A PENDING or NOT_READY gate stops routing.
